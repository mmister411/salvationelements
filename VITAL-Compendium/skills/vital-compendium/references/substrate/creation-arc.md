#### Creation Arc

The alethic fulcrum of all creation is the historical event of the crucifixion, burial and resurrection of the incarnate Logos, Logic Capital L, co-equal, co-eternal, uncreated Son of God.

In the beginning, God created. By faith we understand that the universe was formed at God’s command, so that what is seen was not made out of what was visible. He is before all things, and in Him all things hold together. God looked upon all that He had made, and indeed, it was very good.

God gave the world to Man. Man sinned, bringing death and decay. Suffering became the default experience, requiring labor, collaboration, and sacrifice to avoid, until God makes all things new.

Physical and spiritual forces have stake in the corruption of creation. Malice, idolatry, treachery and exploitation abound new and remixed repeatedly, sometimes accelerating the spread and effects of corruption. The Fall disrupted creation's original connection to God's inexhaustible provision, under which the created order possessed conditions of sustained vitality unavailable to its subsequent condition. Physical creation thereafter operates under progressive decay, energetic depletion, and biological degradation, including the deterioration of genetic integrity through the propagation of life. The generation of human souls continues according to God's design, though the condition of human existence is not untouched by corruption.

According to the counsel of His own will, God intervened globally with the Flood as a population and geological reset, approx 3298 BCE, promising never again to intervene with a global flood.

After the deluge, population recovered, spiritual antagonism regrouped, early civilization spread, and knowledge of God faded through man’s deprioritization. The tower at Babel was raised for the centralizing of the exalting of Man around 2700 BC.

God intervened and foiled the success of the tower at Babel, architecting languages and families as early nation states with spiritual principalities to resist a self-destructive unity of man before its allotted time.

God called Abram to become Abraham as the father of many believers and many nations, and an important linchpin in the progression of the redemption of creation.

God forged the nation of Israel apart from the nations divided at the judgment of Babel, as a nation of His own among the peoples of the world and as a flex against the kingdom of darkness.

Jesus, the Word, by the operation of the Holy Spirit according to the will of the Father, incarnated into history as the seed of the woman, Son of David, Lion of Judah, Holy One of Israel, and Lamb of God slain from the foundation of the world. Before Abraham was, He Is, and only through the finished work of Jesus can anyone escape perdition. He lived sinlessly, was executed innocently, buried in a sealed tomb, raised by power, ascended to power, and will return in power to judge every person impartially according to their works, granting the life He alone secured to those whom He wills.
