#### Humans

The human soul persists consciously beyond bodily death, retaining personal identity, awareness, memory, and the capacity for experience. Its non-mortality bears similarities to the continuing existence of other spiritual beings while retaining the distinctions proper to human personhood and nature.

Tripartite Anthropology - The human person consists of body, soul, and spirit, each distinguishable in nature and operation while together constituting one human being. Human existence, identity, and spiritual condition involve all three, whose distinct capacities and relationships bear upon life, death, corruption, and redemption.

Human testimony is a valid evidentiary pathway to knowledge of reality. Its content deserves substantive assessment according to its claims, coherence, circumstances, and available evidence, without presuming fault, deception, or incapacity in the witness. Testimony carries informational value in its own right, and its truth is determined by correspondence with reality.

No accountable human is self-representatively innocent, vanishingly few are volitionally villainous, and every single soul from miscarriage to minister to maniac has intrinsic value through bearing the image of God.
