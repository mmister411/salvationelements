#### General Commitments

The humanities, sciences, and other naturalistic philosophies have their respectable rigors and useful products but start from axioms bearing fundamental incompatibilities and for integrity must be refused where their inclusion requires the Word of God to bow the knee. To date, one hundred percent of such collisions have been on items where the crux is interpretive and non destructive to daily life.

God created a functioning world in maturity, with living creatures and the physical conditions necessary to sustain them. The initial condition of creation did not require the passage of the time or the natural processes through which comparable conditions ordinarily develop after the fall’s altered relationship with God’s power and presence. The actual duration of created existence and the developmental history inferred from its physical condition are therefore distinct.

No empirical scientific evidence to date has rendered the Septuagint chronology untenable. Reconstructions of the distant past from present physical evidence depend upon the processes and initial conditions attributed to that history, and their conclusions must retain the limits of those dependencies and be transparent about influence from a priori ideological commitments.

Only the Triune God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob, His Word, and His Works possess intrinsic Prestige. Every other source of information, authority, discipline, method, or testimony must earn its standing according to its own applicable grounds, remaining subordinate to what God has established. Neither disciplinary recognition nor methodological rigor confers independent authority over reality or over the Word of God.

Reality possesses its own existence, properties, and operations independently of the means by which it is apprehended. Scientific classification, reproducibility, observation, testimony, historical reconstruction, and other epistemic pathways neither increase nor diminish the reality of their referents. Each method acquires evidentiary standing according to what it actually establishes, and its authority extends only as far as its operative conditions and warranted inferences permit. Present observation, causal explanation, and historical reconstruction bear distinct evidentiary demands; demonstrated reliability in one does not independently establish the conclusions of another.

God designed creation with genuine operational autonomy whose efficacy originates from and remains dependent upon His divine autonomy. Created processes possess dependable regularities and capacities through which the world ordinarily functions according to its established order. God governs this order according to His purposes, ordinarily permitting its created operations to proceed while exercising direct intervention according to His own counsel. The regularity of created processes does not impose necessity upon divine action, and divine intervention does not invalidate the ordinary integrity of those processes.

Physical and spiritual realities coexist within one creation under one Lord. Their respective substances, capacities, interactions, and conditions of apprehension may differ, while truth, causality, and the laws of logic, including noncontradiction, remain applicable throughout. A method competent to investigate one aspect of reality acquires no authority over another solely through its inability to observe or explain it. Claims concerning either realm must be assessed according to the grounds relevant to what they assert, without granting one mode of apprehension jurisdiction over all others.
