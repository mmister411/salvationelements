#### Spiritual Realities

Unclean spirits, tormenting spirits, angels held in chains of darkness, heavenly councils, wheels covered with eyes, unseen angelic hosts, and spiritual principalities exercising influence over earthly affairs are real features of creation. Spiritual beings possess distinct identities, capacities, relationships, and spheres of activity. Their presence and operations may be perceived by human beings and even animals, as Scripture records. Spiritual opposition can produce sustained consequences in earthly circumstances, while heavenly powers may intervene in ways ordinarily hidden from physical sight.

The chambers of death, bars of the deep, outer darkness, spiritual captivity, conscious torment, and wailing and gnashing of teeth are realities disclosed in Scripture. Human beings may perceive spiritual realms and beings while bodily alive, experience consciousness and remembrance beyond bodily death, lift up their eyes in torment, and return from death with memories acquired through experiences beyond ordinary mortal perception. Bodily death and the limitations of physical sensation do not exhaust the possibilities of personal experience or access to spiritual reality.

The heavenly dwelling of God is real and possesses its own created splendor, order, and substance. Streets of glass-like gold, the crystal river of life proceeding from the throne of God, fantastic holy beasts, seraphim, and the other living orders of Heaven belong to the actual glory of that realm. Heaven is free from decay, corruption, and privation; no unmet need, deficiency, or wasting remains there. The life, beauty, holiness, and abundance of that kingdom are not symbolic reductions of spiritual comfort alone, but disclose a true and glorious reality prepared and sustained by God. What is called heaven can have different referents due to the new heavens and new earth but the semiotic field is stable nonetheless. Through trances, dreams, during death that was reversed, people have seen selections of what is to come.
