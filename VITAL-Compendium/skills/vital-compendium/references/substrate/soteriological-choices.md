#### Soteriological Choices

Corporate Preordination and Predestination in Christ - All divine preordination and predestination regarding eternal salvation concern the corporate effects of Christ: the purposes, outcomes, privileges, and inheritances established through Him. These realities are ordained in Christ, while the participation of individual persons in them corresponds to their actual relationship with God and the conditions under which they receive and respond to His grace. Divine predestination or preordering of other sorts in Scripture consistently points to matters not associated with an individual’s disposition in final judgment.

Predestination establishes that the corporate body in Christ inherits through Christ. Individuals participate in that inheritance through continued union with Christ. Those genuinely sanctified by Christ’s blood can profane it through willful rebellion, abandoning Christ, and affairs with corruption unless they repent. God’s promised inheritance remains secure through His integrity, while individual participation remains conditional upon conforming to Christs likeness by grace through enduring faith. All who are in the likeness of Christ will receive inheritance with Christ. All who are in the likeness of corruption are unable to receive that inheritance despite there having been a place made for them.

The making-new of the human spirit is a work of the Holy Spirit, who exercises this regenerative power carte blanche, including apart from participation in the specific covenant of the Cross beginning in time circa 27-33 AD. This work remains rooted in the redemptive purchase of the Lamb slain from before the foundation of the world. The benefits of Christ’s sacrifice extend according to the eternal counsel of God beyond the historical availability and covenantal administration of its revelation within the human timeline.

Dynamic Redemptive Work of the Trinity - The Father, Son, and Holy Spirit accomplish redemption through divine operations responsive to the differing conditions, capacities, histories, and circumstances of human beings. The means and manner of divine engagement vary according to the realities encountered, while the abiding demands of believer-scale agape equivalence and affirmative assent toward the eternal God remain constant. “If you do well, will you not be accepted?”

Universal Access to God through God - God provides every human being with genuine access to Himself. His initiative reaches people within their actual circumstances, including those without historical access to Scripture, the proclamation of the gospel, or the explicit knowledge of Jesus Christ. The distribution of divine revelation and the availability of its written or preached forms do not delimit the reach of God’s Spirit, grace, or redemptive activity. “Is it not lawful to do what I will with my own?”

Revelation, Responsibility, and Divine Judgment - Human responsibility corresponds to the truth received, recognized, and acted upon. Greater revelation confers greater privileges and introduces greater liability for willful rejection. Cases involving minimal revelation, limited moral development, or conditions exceeding what Scripture emphatically discloses belong to God’s internal counsel concerning what is fitting. His judgment accounts perfectly for the actual capacities, knowledge, opportunities, and conduct of each person.

Christ’s finished work prevents inherited Adamic corruption from making human failure automatically decisive. Deliberately embracing corruption remains consequential. Every corruption must finally be cleansed, corrected, or consigned to perdition. Creatures that love corruption, which unfortunately includes those who are on the Vine as branches in Christ and includes many out in the world timeline, cannot be simply cleansed, have become themselves corrupted, and will be cast into perdition. Whoever resists their corruption and presses to light has a real opportunity for escape through the finished work of Christ.

Final Judgment and Differentiated Inheritance - The ultimate judgment has two outcome categories: favorable and unfavorable. Individuals within the favorable outcome need not possess identical covenantal privileges, functions, responsibilities, or inheritances. Divine acceptance and the particular benefits, offices, and relationships granted within that acceptance are distinguishable realities, distributed according to God’s righteous judgment and eternal purposes. An unfavorable eternal outcome is similarly calibrated to the intersection of God’s perfect justice and the works of the person.


Distinct Saving Operations and the Fullness of Grace - Personal sin and inherited corruption create distinct spiritual problems, and God’s saving work addresses each according to its nature. Consequently, exemption from one saving operation does not entail exemption from every saving operation. God withholds only what a person is materially disqualified to receive or what would make Him culpable before His own integrity. God gives 1,000% of the grace that can be given, withholding no possible benevolence compatible with His holiness, righteousness, agape, and eternal purposes.
