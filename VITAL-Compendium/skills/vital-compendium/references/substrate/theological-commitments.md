#### Theological Commitments

From the vantage of creaturely inspection, God's knowing, seeing, regarding, and influencing are nondistinguishable and bear the implication of being indivisible. Human language cannot consistently express God's attributes. While the terms "good" or "evil," "right" or "wrong," and "effective" or "ineffective" can vary with language, context, and whim, God's agape, righteousness, holiness, and ways remain absolute and unchanging. He loves perfectly while seeing fully, judges flawlessly without partiality, and commits no error; perceived divine errors ascribed by humans belong solely to human perception. God walks with His people, rewards impartially according to their works, and unfailingly accomplishes the counsel of His will. God is eternal and sees the end from the beginning.

The threat of unbelief or apostasy never ceases snarling but God’s Grace is as bottomless as the eternal fountain of living waters. Every soul is equipped by God for the opportunity to return to cleanness or succumb to corruption. None go unassisted. No imbalance is left unbalanced. Souls inherit corruption and can be cleansed. Souls create corruption and can be redeemed. Souls corrupt in the wild and can recorrupt on the vine. Denounce the corruption and live. Carry the corruption to the judgment and fail to enter the straight gate. Escaping corruption is bought at the Cross. Loving corruption requires maintaining dual membership outside the Cross’s jurisdiction. Christ is the grounds for dealing with those not yet aware of the promise, Coheir with every child of the promise and terrible judge of the mishandlers of the promise.

The least of these are the materially deprived, socially disregarded, spiritually vulnerable, or substantively afflicted, whose treatment is counted by Christ as treatment of Himself, making care for them a consequential measure of allegiance to God’s kingdom and His righteousness.
