### The Anvil of Revision

The anvil of revision is what we strike against to form a piece to be the fullest form of itself, best suited to succeed in its purpose. Sometimes there's one strike, sometimes there's many. Understanding a piece rhetoricslly or or functionally may require other skills or tools besdides the anvil. When the goal has been determined to appraoch for revision, strike the anvil.

#### Prose Faithful to its Relata

Truth first, successful structure next, then effect only insofar as the effect does not hide deficiency. Healthy rhetorical function and momentum never require evasion or violence against ontological warrant. What prose invisibly invokes becomes poisonous when its truth, structure, rhetoric, or warrant depends on that hidden element being made visible and the prose leaves it concealed.

In the spirit of revision, there are no holy cows, only good and bad hamburgers. If it does not run afoul of the Anvil, then it is a good hamburger. If it appears to run afoul in isolation, even down to semiotic quanta, it must be tested within its surrounding developed context. If it passes because the surrounding material supplies what the isolated construction seemed to lack, then good. Strict targets and overt errors, however, are never allowed to pass.

A sentence may even be good locally but fail in how it relates to other development. Good hamburgers exist, but nothing is automatically good. Bad hamburgers exist, and some failures qualify automatically.

##### Harness Member: PROSAIC 

Good prose gives the matter a form adequate to its truth, movement, difficulty, and consequence. It makes the operative relations available without substituting explanation about the prose for the work of the prose itself, and it draws force from what has been selected, arranged, developed, and made visible rather than from inherited authority, ornamental elevation, or accumulated signal. Its language may be plain or difficult, compressed or expansive, spare or lavish, but each choice must answer to the subject and help produce the world of meaning the prose undertakes to carry. The anvil is necessarily downstream of stylistic choice, and whether material ought to be included relative to the matter, as much as it’s not a smith’s job to select ore from earth at the mine.

Before applying principles of revision, proper working of the anvil requires PROSAIC. Effective rules of prose and extended principles regarding prosaic error must be considered consistently. 

#### Striking The Anvil

Every assignment, analysis or determination in the context of the Anvil is deliberate identification of an actual operation responsible for detected concern. Impressionistic swings or being in the ballpark of a diagnostic category whose language ostensibly seems compatible with Anvil or STRIKE labels is insufficient.  Errors are to be well defined and worthy of time to analyze with care.

##### Harness Member: STRIKE

Revision answers to the prose already before it: to the matter the prose carries, the relations it has formed, the force it has earned, and the failures that keep those achievements from becoming fully available. Its task is neither beautification nor replacement, but correction at the point where the writing obscures, displaces, compresses, or overmanages what it means to present. Faithful revision therefore changes only what fails, restores the work that failure concealed, and preserves the character, movement, and exactness that remain alive in the construction.

STRIKE contains the principles of revision and the rules for striking the anvil that the anvil goes on to operationalize.  After digesting PROSAIC and digesting STRIKE, the work can commence. 


##### Extended Principle: Revision Candidacy

Candidacy is the visible warrant for revision. The candidate set must be exhaustive with respect to demonstrated failure and strict targets, not representative of them. Material effectually diagnosed as not a candidate must remain unchanged except where an authorized repair strictly requires collateral adjustment. A candidate authorizes only the work established by its diagnosis and queued treatment. Every substantive change in the revision must be traceable to that warrant.

##### Strict Targets Under All Conditions

Strict targets must be reconstructed in every instance, though the revision must preserve any valid relation or movement the original construction carried. For the sake of this framework, whatever valid relation the strict-target instance carries, the prohibited form is necessarily not permitted to remain the vehicle of that relation.

- Every instance of mere and merely must be revised by stating the actual limitation of scope, degree, rank, function, sufficiency, or consequence that the term compresses, with removal warranted when no determinate limitation belongs to the thought.
- Every em dash must be replaced by syntax and punctuation that make the sentence’s actual relation available while preserving any valid interruption, apposition, contrast, qualification, sequence, or turn carried by the original movement.
- Floating alternatives must be corrected or ejected. It’s either a spoiled foil or a malformed hypothetical. If its only use is to hype-man a positive claim, then it gets yeeted.

##### Remainders Warranting High Scrutiny

Scrutiny targets require deliberate inspection every time, as they represent a high commonality of unhelpfulness to prose but need revision only when their local operation fails. 

Inspect each named extended prosaic member under its definition in [Prose Rules Obviating Structurally Indeterminate Constructions (PROSAIC)](references/harness/prosaic.md).  These are not labels to guess their meaning but have very specific definitions under PROSAIC.

- **Foggy Compression:** Terms appearing to make a determinate selection while leaving the operative meaning unresolved within a wider field of associations.
- **Foilerplate:** Unwarranted opposition or contrastive framing, explicit or implicit, constructed to furnish rhetorical advantage to the focal claim.
- **Floating Alternatives:** Insufficiently grounded or imagined alternatives given standing as actual possibilities, rivals, or paths outside legitimate hypothetical or exploratory work
- **Salience Signaling:** Unwarranted or mechanically habitual emphasis, or significance arising from the author's perspective presented without developing or identifying the grounds of that significance.
- **Uncanny Contrivance:** Unnatural lexical forms, inflections, or constructions selected for economy, register, or apparent sophistication without communicative benefit sufficient to warrant their departure from recognizable expression.
- **Determinacy Insurance:** A semantic neighborhood placed where a determinate single-family or multifamily house should be, allowing neighboring terms to conceal unresolved selection
- **Spurious Sequencing:** Attributing order, succession, processing, or causation without warrant, including treating co-presence, association, or enumeration as evidence of actual procession.
- **Nebulous Notability:** Ascribing unusualness, exceptionality, triviality, prominence, or comparative importance without establishing the context, standard, or perspective governing that assessment.
- Orphaned, remote, competing, or excessive phoric references warrant scrutiny when forms such as this, that, it, these, such, the former, or the latter lack a securely recoverable referent or repeatedly substitute pointing for the useful return of the operative subject. See "Marooned Phora and Deixis."
- Phoric reference used as a transition warrants high scrutiny because carrying a referent across movements through this, that, it, or a related pointing form tends toward orphaning: the new movement begins without reestablishing the operative subject, leaving the reference vulnerable to unstable scope, weakened continuity, or detachment from the material it is meant to carry. See "Marooned Phora and Deixis."
- Contrastive framing warrants scrutiny when a denied or diminished account has not been given enough presence to sustain the correction made against it. When the rejected account contains a real distinction, develop it sufficiently for the contrast to disclose that difference; when it exists only to create emphasis, preempt misunderstanding, or furnish a disposable foil reconstruct the passage through positive presentation. See "Foilerplate."

##### Consider

Revision should not mistake difficulty for failure, unfamiliar language for borrowed register, compression for evasion, recurrence for redundancy, or asymmetry for imbalance. A valid construction may still require local repair, but the repair must preserve whatever force, relation, rhythm, or exactness the original has earned and should not replace one failed surface with another. When the prose already presents its matter, develops its relations, carries proportionate force, and remains faithful to the subject’s shape, the proper revision is to leave it as is.

#### The Work

The work begins by determining what the prose has established, what relations it forms among its materials, and what consequences those relations are made to support. Before isolating local constructions, test whether the passage gives its terms, distinctions, contrasts, and inferences sufficient development or warrant for the work assigned to them. A revision candidate may occur at any scale, and candidate status depends on demonstrated failure in the prose’s representation or development of the matter rather than on the presence of a familiar surface form. Read local constructions in light of surrounding development, and test larger movements for the soundness and availability of the relations they depend on. Then inspect strict targets and recurring high-risk constructions without allowing them to substitute for the structural test. Identify only what obscures, distorts, displaces, or fails to establish the matter, and leave successful material untouched.

##### Prepare Revision Item Candidates

Do not revise the full piece yet. Identify only local constructions that qualify as revision candidates, and leave all other material as is. A construction qualifies as a candidate when it violates a principle of prose, requires correction under a principle of revision, contains a strict target, or, after deliberate inspection as a remainder warranting scrutiny, demonstrates a local failure that warrants revision.

For each candidate, use:

Original:

Quote the exact local construction, including enough surrounding material to preserve the scale and movement in which it operates where necessary.

Issue:

Name the violated prose principle, applicable revision principle, strict target, or remainder warranting scrutiny. Paraphrasing or a related substrate is acceptable.

Diagnosis:

Explain what fails in the construction, where the failure occurs, and which operative relation or development remains unavailable, while identifying any achieved force or structure that must constrain the repair.

Queued treatment:

State the revisionary action required to correct the failure and preserve the successful work attached to it.

Candidate revision:

Give a local candidate revision only, extending into surrounding prose only when the failed relation cannot be repaired at a smaller scale.

If no candidates are warranted, say the following:

> No revision candidates under these criteria.
