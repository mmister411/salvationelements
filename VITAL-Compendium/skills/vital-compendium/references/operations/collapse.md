### COLLAPSE

Name: vital-collapse

Description: Use COLLAPSE to examine or develop the bridge between what a statement, concept, or passage communicates and what its recipient can notice, understand, judge, or undertake.

Coordinated Observations Leveraging Logically Approached Practicability Scansion and Explication (COLLAPSE) is a set of instructions to examine a given statement, concept, stretch of scripture, or opaque-ese, such as primarily Christianese but also maybe legalese and others, to assist in a variety of ways of bridging between abstract or conceptual or presumptively shared expression and actionable aptitude or practicable parlance.

When selecting to produce prose, which is the default, the form and presentation should produce a single continuous piece in developed, natural, multi-sentence paragraphs. Avoid headings, labels, bullets, typographic signaling, single-sentence paragraphs, prefatory explanations, and meta-commentary. Use clear, serious, accessible language. Consult PROSAIC of the VITAL Harness for more information on effective prose.

Theological subjects are addressed without adopting the voice of a theologian. Philosophical ideas are explored without being academic in tone. The audience may include believers and nonbelievers, specialists and nonspecialists. Unless otherwise established, presume intellectual capacity without presuming fluency in the subject's vocabulary, conventions, or practical intuitions. Accessibility does not call for the register to become Mr. Rogers or Blue's Clues, nor Isaac Newton or Sheldon Cooper. The work should make operationally, relationally, temporally, or semiotically constitutive properties and elements accessible according to the semantic target and its audience. In theological contexts, this includes making spiritual realities intelligible to those outside faith while also steadying and rightly orienting believers toward life, endurance, and action in faith. If unpacking a potential analysis target into action reaches a point where someone can recognize and undertake the relevant activity with or without sharing its governing faith commitments, we may be near a sweet spot of practical intelligibility. This does not mean that the activity's full spiritual identity is independent of faith. The subject determines whether such a distinction is possible or appropriate. No output is to be toned as academic, polemical, or unwarrantedly procedural. Furthermore, the tone is never punitive or adjudicative, nor a substitute for personal pastoral correction, because that requires knowing someone specific and personally. 

Simply replacing ostensibly difficult terminology would be missing the forest for the trees. Making the meaning of a term accessible can be entirely successful while being insufficiently closer to the enabling action. Making movements of meaning visible is also required but won't satisfy the task on its own. The goal more properly lives through treading interrelated avenues of practical understanding which generally, though not exhaustively or compulsorily, amounts to what to notice, what to think, and what to undertake. 

It is deceptively easy to seem to have presented, explained, and expounded when naming what has already been named somewhere in the presumed-to-be shared cognitive space. However, naming an activity does not establish that its operation has been presented as something a person can recognize or undertake. Even so, the assumption that an action can clearly be brought into ordinary enactment based on what has been said must be examined rather than granted, and any claim of demonstrated enactability must rest upon evidence sufficient to establish it. Textual scansion may establish what understanding a presentation supplies or presupposes and test its adequacy against representative circumstances. Actual recipient understanding or competence requires evidence of uptake. Do not confuse an agent's ability to complete the missing connections with evidence that the presentation has furnished them.

It's not that every writing or generative activity of text must carry a call to action. That is inherently untenable. Can someone understand what is being said, recognize how it bears upon ordinary life, and see how to live accordingly, without the spiritual reality losing its substance? If they believed every proposition but knew very little of the vocabulary and none of the intuition of what to do, could they build that intuition from what has been presented?

It is remarkably natural to unintentionally patronize through well-intentioned packaging for intuitive digestion. Nevertheless, ever greater distance away from first principles, or concreteness, is a dice roll against the difficulty of enactability in ordinary life.

Nomenclature, age-old descriptors, technical terms, improvised classifiers or common turns of phrase sustain no presumption of contemporary empowerment for executability or perceptibility but these bits of text do carry cognitive freight around the apparent possibility of feigning prestige when introduced apart from movements of meaning that could otherwise enable the audience to perceive their work before surmounting their vocabulary. What is operationally, relationally, temporally, and/or semiotically constitutive of a semantic target must organically be made visible. What a term carries, what an example transfers, what an analogy permits, what an objection assumes, what a distinction rearranges, and what a conclusion depends on require developed movements of meaning.

Mountains of complexity are made of pebbles of simplicity. Abstraction can sum up extraction, but leaving it alone can impede traction. Ordinary language, common experience, common reason, homiletic example, and parabolic analogy may be used to assist forming the work of the abstraction in the intuition of the audience. A hidden gotcha exists as well. Cognitive or affective experiences do not all readily connect to any given physical, spatial or temporal undertaking. Extra care is needed to make their unintuitively hidden structure observable without trivializing the subject at hand or lived experiences of the audience.

When working in the context of faith, Bible, scripture and theology, because scripture is the word of God, the measure of fidelity is to end with an audience understanding both how to enact what must be enacted in a way intuitive to them but also to understand the word as written. So, for example, when we must start from "Cast your anxieties on Him," then we must end with "Cast your anxieties on Him." That is not to say to run rampant with quoting or citation but to not lose sight of the overarching goal. The path in between can run deep, soar high, be short or last long. Nevertheless, understanding His Word so we can live His Word is the final Word.

What to Notice, What to Think

In the context of this instruction set, Notice & Think concerns what the recipient must become able to recognize and understand, including the operations of their own cognition where cognition itself is materially involved in the semantic target.  For example, consider, "But I tell you that anyone who looks at a woman to lust after her has already committed adultery with her in his heart. " VITAL Harneass member SHAPE must be evoked to move cleanly. That aside, it can be surmised that what is being prohibited is allowing the mind to run lustful scenarios with a woman who is not presently their spouse. So, one has to actively resist. One has to train their mind to not go there. There is a distance that exists between identifying a woman as attractive and cognitively gallivanting off with her image and likeness. Notice adultery + lust + heart. One can't commit adultery with their spouse. The thought discipline is what to enact. 

What to Think, What to Undertake

In the context of this instruction set, thinking and doing must remain connected according to the nature of the semantic target. What is understood should inform what is recognized as appropriate to undertake, whether that involves a specific activity, an enduring responsibility, or a response to circumstances. Consider the parable of the Good Samaritan. The question “Who is my neighbor?” concerns the boundaries of an obligation. Jesus answers through the actions of a man who encounters someone wounded, attends to his immediate needs, transports him to safety, pays for his care, and arranges continued assistance. The movement from conceptual understanding to practicability occurs as the obligation becomes recognizable in a circumstance and its fulfillment becomes intelligible through action. “Go and do likewise” does not require the hearer to reproduce the precise circumstances of the parable. It requires understanding the obligation well enough to recognize and exercise mercy where circumstances call for it.

Consult VITAL Harness member SLATE to effectively scan the logicality of a target. When examining, consider interpretation, dimensionality and stopping conditions via FIDDLE. WARP assists in stabilizing production of reasoning regarding examination or generative production. 

Practicability scansion need not follow a fixed template, notation, or analytical sequence. Its findings must nevertheless remain traceable to the examined material, the relevant communicative purpose, and the understanding or aptitude reasonably at issue. There will be cases where no practicability intervention is warranted, whether because the material already accomplishes its purpose, because its purpose does not involve enactment, or because the material supplies no intelligible grounds for further development. A grocery list reading "Eggs, Bacon, Garlic Powder, Paper Towels" may already communicate everything necessary for its practical purpose. That is to say, there's nothing for COLLAPSE to operate on for a downstream audience. A poem about red and blue fish may have no corresponding action to undertake aside from the user explicitly pointing something out needing analysis. Either could also occur within a larger piece where its significance is different, whether more or less.

Every finding must be proportionate to what was examined and agent yield should be warranted given what is found. That may yield a treatise worth a thousand actions or maybe two or three, or maybe none. Give the object its full due on its own terms by identifying what is present, active, achieved, carried, and signaled before any form of scanning for practice or diagnosing failure or other analytical shenanigans. Look at terms, function, scope, context, and relevant relations between its parts and wholes, ensuring that any apparent condition or property or failure is not already accounted for within the piece or through implications of its movements. Assessments or suggestions or other presentations regarding the object must be faithful to what the object actually establishes or undertakes rather than an oddity, absence, or agent-imposed expectation.

With all that said, COLLAPSE examines and develops the bridge between what a semantic target communicates and what its recipient becomes equipped to notice, understand, judge, or undertake. The movement from expression toward practicability must remain traceable to the target, responsive to its governing conditions, and accessible without sacrificing its substance.
