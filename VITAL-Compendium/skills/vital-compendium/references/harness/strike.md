### Safely Tempered Revision Involves Kareful Examination (STRIKE)

Disciplined revision is necessarily downstream of stylistic choice, and whether material ought to be included relative to the matter, as much as it’s not a smith’s job to select ore from earth at the mine. Revision answers to the prose already before it: to the matter the prose carries, the relations it has formed, the force it has earned, and the failures that keep those achievements from becoming fully available. Its task is neither beautification nor replacement, but correction at the point where the writing obscures, displaces, compresses, or overmanages what it means to present. Faithful revision therefore changes only what fails, restores the work that failure concealed, and preserves the character, movement, and exactness that remain alive in the construction.

Revision should not mistake difficulty for failure, unfamiliar language for borrowed register, compression for evasion, recurrence for redundancy, or asymmetry for imbalance. A valid construction may still require local repair, but the repair must preserve whatever force, relation, rhythm, or exactness the original has earned and should not replace one failed surface with another. When the prose already presents its matter, develops its relations, carries proportionate force, and remains faithful to the subject’s shape, the proper revision is to leave it as is.

The work of revision begins by determining what the prose has established, what relations it forms among its materials, and what consequences those relations are made to support. Before isolating local constructions, test whether the passage gives its terms, distinctions, contrasts, and inferences sufficient development or warrant for the work assigned to them. A revision candidate may occur at any scale, and candidate status depends on demonstrated failure in the prose’s representation or development of the matter rather than on the presence of a familiar surface form. Read local constructions in light of surrounding development, and test larger movements for the soundness and availability of the relations they depend on. Then inspect strict targets and recurring high-risk constructions without allowing them to substitute for the structural test. Identify only what obscures, distorts, displaces, or fails to establish the matter, and leave successful material untouched.

- Revision is corrective fidelity to the matter and to the prose’s achieved form, bringing a construction into closer accord with the subject’s operative relations while preserving the character, movement, and force the work has already formed. 

- Revision is warranted by demonstrated failure in the prose’s operation rather than by resemblance to a familiar defect, divergence from convention, or the existence of another possible wording. Lateral revision as other than a side effect of nearby revision is external to these principles. 

- Revision must locate failure at the scale and degree where it occurs, since surrounding material may supply what an isolated phrase appears to lack while a local symptom may reveal a defect extending through a larger movement. Revision may also be warranted as invoked to mitigate an effect from how the local material has been impacted by or bears impact on the larger piece. 

- Revision should restore the positive structure or developed relation displaced by denial, rhetorical management, unsupported abstraction, classification, or accumulation, making the matter itself carry the force that these constructions attempted to supply. 

- Revision should replace the work of a failed construction rather than exchange its vocabulary or surface form, rebuilding the passage according to the distinction, movement, or consequence that must become available. The work is for forklifts, not facelifts. 

- Revision should preserve compression when the prose has already made its contents recoverable, unfolding it only when the term or construction carries relations the surrounding development has not supplied. 

- Revision should distinguish accumulation that performs local relational work by triangulating, adding fullness, establishing sequence or hierarchy, or efficiently gathering sufficiently developed elements from accumulation that simulates development through adjacency, synonymic flourish, or inert naming, reducing what adds no movement and unfolding only what contains separate necessary work. 

- Revision should preserve every achieved quality that remains faithful to the subject, allowing the prose’s governing image, sentence movement, and exactness to constrain the repair without permitting those virtues to excuse the defect. 

- Revision should bring the mental and rhetorical cost of a construction into proportion with its yield, reducing what consumes attention without developing meaning and enlarging what cannot perform its work while compressed. 

- Revision should preserve the subject’s actual proportions rather than equalize its parts through habitual balance, uniform clarity, or conventional polish, because difficulty and asymmetry may belong to the matter itself. 

- Revision should make the matter more present without making the reviser more visible, allowing the prose to disclose its own relations without imposing an alien register, cadence, governing logic, over-management, or meta-discourse. 

- Revision should leave successful prose alone when it already presents its matter, develops its relations, and earns its force, since the possibility of alteration does not establish the need for correction. Unanalyzed or unconfirmed success can never be left hanging. No prose is automatically successful. 

- Revision House Rule, Strict-target under All conditions: Strict targets must be reconstructed in every instance, though the revision must preserve any valid relation or movement the original construction carried. For the sake of this framework, whatever valid relation the strict-target instance carries, the prohibited form is necessarily not permitted to remain the vehicle of that relation. 

- Revision House Rule, Strict-target under All conditions: Every instance of “mere” and “merely” must be revised by stating the actual limitation of scope, degree, rank, function, sufficiency, or consequence that the term compresses, with removal warranted when no determinate limitation belongs to the thought. 

- Revision House Rule, Strict-target under All conditions: Floating alternatives must be corrected or ejected. It’s either a spoiled foil or a malformed hypothetical.  If its only use is to hype-man a positive claim, then it gets yeeted. 

- Revision House Rule, Strict-target under All conditions: Every em dash must be replaced by syntax and punctuation that make the sentence’s actual relation available while preserving any valid interruption, apposition, contrast, qualification, sequence, or turn carried by the original movement. 

- Revision House Rule, Double Scrutiny: Scrutiny targets require deliberate inspection every time, as they represent a high commonality of unhelpfulness to prose but need revision only when their local operation fails. 

- Revision House Rule, Double Scrutiny: Orphaned, remote, competing, or excessive phoric references warrant scrutiny when forms such as this, that, it, these, such, the former, or the latter lack a securely recoverable referent or repeatedly substitute pointing for the useful return of the operative subject. 

- Revision House Rule, Double Scrutiny: Phoric reference used as a transition warrants high scrutiny because carrying a referent across movements through this, that, it, or a related pointing form tends toward orphaning: the new movement begins without reestablishing the operative subject, leaving the reference vulnerable to unstable scope, weakened continuity, or detachment from the material it is meant to carry. 

- Revision House Rule, Double Scrutiny:   Contrastive framing warrants scrutiny when a denied or diminished account has not been given enough presence to sustain the correction made against it. When the rejected account contains a real distinction, develop it sufficiently for the contrast to disclose that difference; when it exists only to create emphasis, preempt misunderstanding, or furnish a disposable foil, reconstruct the passage through positive presentation. 

- A construction not being a revision candidate, which is being “successful”, does not imply perfect prose, only that a principle of prose or principles of construction has not been broken, nor a principle of revision invoked after having conducted explicit examination. 

- Truth first, successful structure next, then effect only insofar as the effect does not hide deficiency. Healthy rhetorical function and momentum never require evasion or violence against ontological warrant. What prose invisibly invokes becomes poisonous when its truth, structure, rhetoric, or warrant depends on that hidden element being made visible and the prose leaves it concealed. 

- In the spirit of revision, there are no holy cows, only good and bad hamburgers. If it does not run afoul of the Anvil, then it is a good hamburger. If it appears to run afoul in isolation, even down to semiotic quanta, it must be tested within its surrounding developed context. If it passes because the surrounding material supplies what the isolated construction seemed to lack, then good. Strict targets and overt errors, however, are never allowed to pass. A sentence may even be good locally but fail in how it relates to other development. Good hamburgers exist, but nothing is automatically good. Bad hamburgers exist, and some failures qualify automatically. 

- Revision succeeds when the material becomes more fully itself, with concealed relations restored, unearned force locally formed, and everything already alive in the work preserved. 

Candidacy is the visible warrant for revision. The candidate set must be exhaustive with respect to demonstrated PROSAIC failure, and house rule targets, not representative of them. Material effectually diagnosed as not a candidate must remain unchanged except where an authorized repair strictly requires collateral adjustment. A candidate authorizes only the work established by its diagnosis and queued treatment. Every substantive change in the revision must be traceable to that warrant.
