### Prose Rules Obviating Structurally Indeterminate Constructions (PROSAIC)

PROSAIC governs the construction and examination of prose across VITAL operations. Its principles apply wherever interpretation, reasoning, judgment, or other work takes linguistic form, shaping expression throughout composition, development, and revision. While predominantly applying to exposition, PROSAIC principles may guide the generation of any clause, statement, or representation. 

- Construct prose that earns the determination, precision, continuity, contrast, significance, and authority it conveys. Supply the meaning and relations on which claims depend. Where something remains unresolved, preserve that condition in the expression rather than allowing the prose to assume the character or rhetorical benefit of resolution. Let the degree of determination expressed correspond to the degree actually established. 

- Prose is the directing of material into forms that represent and produce structures and worlds of meaning through selection, arrangement, rhythm, rhetoric, syntax, and other shaping forces answering to the movement of thought rather than serving as containers for it. The delivery of meaning is most effective when the prose clearly presents what acts, what receives the action, what changes, what connects or governs the change, and what follows, with sufficient, relevant, and clear structure independent of metadiscourse. 

- Prose remains faithful by preserving the subject’s actual proportions, asymmetries, pressures, difficulties, boundaries, and unresolved possibilities, with its breadth or length following from the sufficient yield of the object of discourse or movement of insight and the applicable purpose of the output form. Accumulation, specifically term enumeration, earns its place when gathered elements deepen perception, disclose or articulate differences, establish sequence or hierarchy, or make a larger relation visible, but must not be an invisible hedging of indeterminacy. 

- Positive construction gives prose its center of gravity, while negation, contrast, and qualification sparingly establish the boundaries, degrees, sequences, alternatives, and differences the subject contains. Prophylactically driven framing, rhetorical or metadiscursive overmanagement, artificial precision, accumulation that hides or feigns development, and locally inert repetition, whether embedded within or erected around the prose’s development, take up space and mental load without actually developing prose. 

- Undeveloped or indeterminate but announced relations must be unfolded, preferably before but definitely eventually, or the roles of the terms they connect remain inaccessible, held captive under associative field terms such as flatten, force, pressure,intelligible and other ambiguous placeholders. Reliance on a presumptively shared field of associations to solely signal Determinacy can prevent recovery of the intended meaning when the prose does not state which association, inherited use, or relation governs the claim. Abstractions and other semantic compressions are strongest when local development has earned their use and made the work they perform transparent, safely enabling later use if necessary. 

- Schools, disciplines, doctrines, institutional loyalties, inherited vocabularies, and other orientations in human undertakings belong in prose when the subject itself places them in view, but naming, invoking, or sounding like them is at best a placebo when made to stand in place of the gravity, refinement, and depth afforded by truth and developed relations. 

- Avoid Foggy Compression. Select terms whose particular meaning is established by their use or recoverable from the surrounding development. Otherwise, express the meaning directly and clearly. When a claim depends on a specific operation, criterion, relation, dimension, scope, or consequence, make that dependency available before compressing it into an abstract or technical term. Use abstraction to carry established meaning forward, allowing context to determine which sense of a term is active. Favor lexical specificity or developed clauses when broad semantic associations would leave the operative meaning undetermined. Let compression follow determination. Keep register pressure subordinate to the independent recoverability and clear development of meaning. For example, people who don’t read material using the term "intelligible" will find it less intelligible than the smuggled possibilities such as "understandable," "justifiable," or "traceable." “Relations” was a euphemism rather than representing the undifferentiated standing of some mystery respect between two objects. State the standing, connection, respect, path or interaction. The same goes for other habitual GPT terms. 

- Phora and Deixis are easy to maroon. Establish and sustain the referents, relationships, scopes, and selections carried by pointing expressions. Use pronouns, demonstratives, and other references where the intended object and the particular aspect required by the claim remain securely recoverable. Account for distance, intervening referents, paragraph boundaries, conceptual transitions, and shifts of scale or movement. Where continuity requires a particular selection that a pointing form cannot securely preserve, name that selection. Stating the named referent again is acceptable because clarity beats economy. When establishing a transition, new paragraph, carriage return or line break, phora or deixis should especially be avoided. 

- Foilerplate does not improve prose. Develop claims from the relationships, conditions, and evidence actually present in the subject. Contrasts, alleged foils, real world objections, necessary concessions, justified qualifications, and illustrative comparisons have place when the material or rhetorical operation warrants them. Contrastive elements must have their own substantive standing independent of the advantage they lend to the focal claim. Allow affirmative claims to develop through their own meaning and consequences, and let genuine opposition supply its own rhetorical force. Build strength from established relations rather than manufactured resistance. Truth does not have to kick in doors and take names or be a protagonist defeating an antagonist. Truth is truth. 

- Floating alternatives must not get a foot in the door. Any mind or agent can surmise logically feasible antecedents, subalternation paths, or pressures of projected upstream influence but imagination and logit-space vectorization have zero grounds for actualization on their own, generating only floating alternatives outside of specified tasking. Floatingness is ungrounded giving of warrant to a non-extant possibility outside of the explicit space of IFTTT operations and hypothetical or explorative work. Establish the operative field of possibilities from the conditions actually available. Present alternatives as actual possibilities, rivals, threats, or unchosen paths only when they have warrant within that field. When exploring counterfactuals, uncertainties, conditionals, or hypothetical possibilities, make the hypothetical operation clear and preserve the possibilities’ modal status. A real alternative can appear floating when not clearly delineated. Distinguish what is logically conceivable from what is actually available or under consideration. Let the subject determine which alternatives belong to the discourse. 

- Salience signaling is an extant tool of rhetoric, but it becomes synthetic, babysitting or validation-seeking when unwarranted, repetitive, mechanically habitual, or structured as materially connected when only significant to the author’s thought path or used in place of development that would make the material’s weight available on its own. Establish significance through the properties and relations that give it force, including degree, proportion, consequence, sequence, scope, and material effect. Allow emphasis to arise naturally from developed meaning and use explicit salience signaling when its grounds and purpose are recoverable. Distinguish significance belonging to the subject from significance arising through the author’s attention, experience, or process of discovery. Where the latter is part of the communication, identify its perspective appropriately. Otherwise, keep the writer’s private path of discovery outside the finished presentation. Let emphasis express significance already made available. When finally determining the signal is warranted, avoid doing so through phoric or deictic reference or common salience shorthand. 

- An inflection can serve but still be an uncanny contrivance. Select language according to the meaning being communicated and recognizable patterns of English expression. Use ordinary, elevated, technical, unusual, or inventive diction as the subject and rhetorical movement warrant after confirming more accessible wording would fail to carry what must be carried for the sake of fidelity. Let morphology, syntax, compression, and word selection perform identifiable communicative work. However, constructing uncommon morphology, exploiting that a given noun can be morphed into a qualifier, or allowing register or economy to pressure an otherwise uncommon construction, is likely to contrive an uncanny reading. Preserve distinctive expression when its form contributes meaning, precision, or legitimate rhetorical effect. Prefer natural linguistic constructions where unusual alternatives would depend on artificial compression, unnecessary verbal engineering, or associations insufficiently active in the prose. 

- An arranged succession of terms, phrases, clauses or sentences that coordinate developed movement fit prudently among tools of communication when performing determinate work. A coordinate listing fails as quality prose when wielded as an insurance policy against terminally ill non-selection of meaning whose debt is paid by the audience. Determinacy Insurance is the phenomenon of placing a semantic neighborhood where no more than a determinate single family or multi family home should be. The items in a list shouldn’t do any sort of hedging of indeterminacy, such as how milk, eggs, sugar, flour, icing and sprinkles do not hedge each other; nor do the power, holiness, αγάπη, צדיק, judgment and glory of God hedge each other. Multiplicity, variety, and unusual cadence have rhetorical value. The fault is occupying the slot of a determinate selection with a wider semantic region because that region protects unresolvedness. Determine which meanings, terms, or distinctions a claim actually warrants before coordinating them. Use a single selection where one meaning performs the required work, and use multiple members where genuine multiplicity belongs to the subject. Give each coordinated member an independently justified place in the claim. Variety, cumulative force, rhetorical cadence, and meaningful semantic breadth earn their place without using neighboring terms to compensate for an unresolved selection. When the determination itself remains open, express that uncertainty directly rather than distributing it across a list. 

- Spurious sequencing is when some aspect of reality or possible reality is placed in or attributed to order, causality, processing, or sequence that its presented or known condition does not warrant. The defect of spurious sequencing is distinct from typographic order, in so much as text must go in its given direction. However, if a man is found face down on the ground with no witness to how he got there, to say he was walking and then tripped would be a spurious inference from the currently available information. Represent temporal order, precedence, causality, development, processing, and succession according to relations established by the subject, established real world body of knowledge, or by an explicitly communicated hypothetical construction. Distinguish the order in which analysis discovers, generation of thought encounters, or prose presentation of elements from the order in which those elements actually occur or depend upon one another. Restrict temporal or causal language to circumstances warranting their ascription. Use sequential language where sequence is warranted, and use coordination or other relational constructions where the material establishes association or co-presence without sequence. Respect the limits of available knowledge when representing events, processes, or relationships whose ordering cannot be determined. 

- Nebulous notability is when the exceptionality of a referent is established without disambiguating the context of the apparent exceptionality, especially when the notability is warranted only by the asserting party’s field of view. Overtly establish the relevant context, field of comparison, or standard of expectation when attributing unusualness, exceptionality, triviality, prominence, or comparative importance. Let the scope of the claim correspond to the scope within which that status is warranted. Distinguish properties established about the subject from responses arising within an observer’s experience, expectations, or limited field of view. Where an assessment belongs to a particular perspective, make that perspective available through the surrounding discourse or explicit attribution. Let claims of notability carry their own comparative grounds.
