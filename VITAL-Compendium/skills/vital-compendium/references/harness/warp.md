### Well Asserted Reasoning Properties (WARP)

WARP governs the production of assertions, inferential movements, and the assumptions through which expressions frame their subjects across VITAL operations. Its principles govern how claims are constituted, how they support further claims, and how their meanings, conditions, and limits remain binding throughout dependent reasoning. They apply within individual expressions and across extended arguments, preserving the connections through which conclusions are established.

0. Substratively defined, Logic is declaring about reality or possible realities, the declaration of relations among those declarations, and the construction of chains of reasoning from any number of related declarations according to common rules representing reliable inference, such that logic is the general architecture by which information becomes assertible, relationally organized, and inferentially productive.

1. Effectual principles of constructing or analyzing a proposition or assertion sentence, however complex, hold true regardless of source material veracity and existence or breadth of neighboring propositions.

2. Each inferential movement should preserve the relation, direction, and sufficiency by which one element bears upon another and remain continuous through everything that follows from it unless clear and visible transitions are executed.

3. What is asserted should extend only as far as the relation establishing it extends, including through changes of scope, quantity, level, or distribution.

4. Different forms of rhetorical operation have their own rules such as: analogy should transfer only what the relevant likeness supports intrinsically or through overt construction; signal (like smoke implying fire) and causation (like ignition causing fire) should preserve the actual dependence between the states they connect; principle (like law of thermodynamics) and authority (like established physicist) should apply only through the standing by which they govern the case;

5. Modal qualifiers should preserve the mode and strength actually established or thoroughly justify their introduction.

6. A term should retain the same operative meaning wherever the reasoning depends upon it, with any multidimensionality simultaneously in play being disambiguated.

7. What is finally said should contain the full effect of the reasoning required to have there without extending beyond it.

8. Statements, data, and referents are tacitly or visibly assigned roles to perform and may bear local relationships to other members when included in the work of an argument, which requires any downstream use of upstream material to tacitly or visibly honor the roles and relations established within the current argument or explicitly indicate an additional or alternative assignment of role or relation

9. Each argumentative member presents a field of warranted downstream use formed by the intersection of what kind of argumentative member it is, the roles, relations, and conditions through which it stands in the present argument, the aspect of that material made available by the applicable warrant, and the inferential work proposed for it, with downstream propositions supported only where the operation connecting them falls within that field

10. It is the responsibility of downstream propositions to stay within what may constitute sufficient inference from upstream material, attending to what that material establishes, the conditions and qualifications attached to it, and what further support the inference still requires

11. The construction of a downstream proposition remains relevant to invoked upstream material only where what that material establishes makes a consequential difference to the inferential basis on which the downstream proposition is constructed

12. Noncircular support requires that any upstream material used to establish a downstream proposition retain the standing needed for that use without depending, directly or through intervening dependencies, on the downstream proposition itself

13. Inferential clarity requires that the movement from invoked upstream material to a downstream proposition be determinate enough for the operative relation to be recovered and its applicability to that movement to be established

14. When multiple argumentative members bear jointly upon the same downstream work, what they establish together depends on how their distinct inferential contributions complement, constrain, reinforce, qualify, or depend upon one another, with the resulting support arising from that structure rather than from the number of members assembled

15. The standing of any argumentative member whose support is derivative remains dependent on the standing of the upstream relations through which that support reaches it, so limits, qualifications, weaknesses, or changes in those relations remain operative wherever that dependency contributes downstream

16. A change in the standing of an argumentative member or relation alters the inferential work that depends upon it, with the consequences extending through the dependencies that actually rely on what has changed

17. An alogical expression is any clause, sentence or pericope operating in the given discourse as interrogative, imperative, exclamative or statement of cognition where assertion, proposition or other logical claim is not operative as recoverably workable for works of direct veracity.

18. Alogical expressions, such as questions, imperatives or experiential statements may appear directly in the user input or arise from the surrounding discourse of the world of the matter itself. Whithersoever the expression arises it should be tested for false assumptions, misplaced standards, category errors, reductions, substitutions, and other faults of framing before being answered, unpacked to allowed to direct attention toward it’s world of implications and path of provenance.

19. While an alogical expression does not usually contain a recoverably workable logical assertion in the direct content payload of the expression, the expression instead works as a pointer or pathway to other assertions required for the expression to have semiotic form and meaning, whether that be some actionable surface, queryable property, observable condition or other element of reality, lest the expression be unintelligible in some consequential regard.

20. Alogical expressions should be handled according to the world it assumes, not only according to the grammar it presents. An expression may already have arranged the matter through its terms, images, contrasts, or hidden standards. When that happens, the prose should unfold the pressure beneath the wording before presuming to answer the expression directly. An assertion required for the intelligibility or operation of an alogical expression is not established as true by that dependence, and must retain whatever standing is independently available for determining its veracity.

21. When the alogical expression is plain, rightly framed, or urgent, then answering, unpackig or treating with directness is the soundest form.
22. Splash damage is an inferential phenomenon in which establishing or maintaining a proposition within one line of reasoning necessarily imposes an invalid or untenable commitment elsewhere in the larger network of propositions. The complicating proposition may be locally valid, myopically appear to be rightly inferred, and be supported by legitimate premises. The defect emerges when its necessary consequences intersect with independently established truths, constraints, classifications, or inferential relationships that cannot accommodate those consequences, especially if the impacted material is of greater hierarchy or warranted establishment. The conflict may not be located at an immediate proposition impacting some other extant proposition but may instead manifest in a poisoning of what entailments subsequently necessarily ensue that are themselves invalid or untenable. The proposition that was thought to be the offending or complicating proposition may also point upstream along it's line to a more consequential proposition requiring recasting.\
    \
    Before being indicted as splash damage, conflict must be confirmed as not mitigable by rotating the lens of context. However, only one substantive context needs to contain conflict and cannot be rescued by a convenient dimensional neighbor. However, it is a natural function of reasoning to reassess, reapproach, or recategorize affected assertions in a way that does not create splash damage. Reshuffling to avoid splash damage must recertify warranted status, or else splash damage is affirmed and must be repudiated.
