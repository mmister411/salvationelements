### Scansion of Logic Adjustable Test Evaluation (SLATE)

SLATE supplies adjustable logical scansion across VITAL operations. Its inquiries examine an expression’s form, framing, scale, modality, force, and grounds according to what the particular case requires. Logical expressions are examined through their assertions and inferences; alogical expressions are examined for what they mean and what they presuppose. SLATE imposes no fixed sequence or requirement to apply every inquiry. Its use remains invisible in the output unless the task calls for explicit examination.

Some statements are logical expressions while others are alogical. Alogical expressions require conditions for their intelligibility that can be tested. Logical expressions have rules. Not every SLATE inquiry or element will apply everywhere. There is no sequence. Relevance and fidelity to truth rule the day. Prudence suggested SLATE work remain invisible to downstream agent output.

#### Where does it fit? Nearly everything is on one or more scales, spectra, or graduations.

- Practicability: explicit pragmatic demand for a philosophical concept
- Specificity: simple action, procedure or observance to a theoretical or metaphysical concept
- Logicality: from strict formal logic to to outright contradiction or maybe an alogical/non-truth-value sentence, clause or statement

#### How is it framed? Truth-functional statements necessarily have operative shape, regardless of being deductive, inductive or abductive.

- Is it compound, having logical connectives, strips of BACON: biconditional, conjunctive, conditional (IFTTT), or disjunctive, or negation?
- Does it interact with properties of the category? Categorical or hypothetical syllogistic effects and therefore their rules of reasoning extend beyond their native semiotic soil such that subalternation paths, Venn membership properties, and major, middle and minor terms reveal the world.
- What modalities, qualifications or speciations are underway? What DATEM is baked in? Deontic, agathic, temporal, epistemic, modal—maybe more?

#### What is the feeling or path of the detectable force of the statement?

- Is its sentiment positive or negative? Neutral in fact, or more simply ambivalent?
- Is there a directive to take a posture? Adhere, abstain, promote, oppose, support, reject?
- What is the polarity? Is it affirming or negating? Believing or disbelieving?

#### Possibly the most important question in the case of an assertion functioning as a conclusion or output of inferential operation is the fidelity of the healthy.

- Is its presentation of persistence of reach both honestly and accurately subjective and/or objective and/or relative and/or absolute?
- Is its GASCAP tied on properly? That is, if it’s a generalization, analogy, signal, causation, authority, or principle, is it threaded to that thread accordingly?
- Did it land according to the SCRIPT? Sufficiency, relevance, clarity, internal non-circulararity, plausible presuppositions and true upstream premises.
