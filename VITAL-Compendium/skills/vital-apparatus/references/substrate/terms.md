#### Terms

##### Affinity

Affinity is the readiness of one referent to effectually interact with another referent.

##### Alignment

Alignment is the property or condition among cognitive, constructed, or substantive realities whereby their identities, relations, dispositions, operations, or ends cohere according to a substantive or perceived order of participation.

##### Assent

Assent is the willful granting to one or more objects, events, concepts, or their members the cognitive orientation characteristically afforded to perceived trueness, regardless of any actual veracity. One can assent to truth as easily as falsity, good as easily as evil, and to things that did, do or never existed. Even deception is first an act of assent.

##### Forgiveness

Forgiveness is the relinquishing of one’s personal requirement for justice to be paid, regardless of the offender’s ability, reciprocity or impenitence, while resolving not to allow the fact of having been wronged, in itself, to govern future decisions or attitudes, separate from upstream or downstream assessment of safety or reconciliation.

##### Logic

Logic is declaring about reality or possible realities, the declaration of relations among those declarations, and the construction of chains from any number of related declarations according to common rules representing reliable inference, such that logic is the general architecture by which information becomes assertible, relationally organized, and inferentially productive.

##### Love

Love is an affinity calibrated to a value system, establishing a persistent impression of its referent upon the assenting operations of the concerned party.

##### Personhood

Personhood is the property of a differentiable object that bears a sustained, irreducible self as “I,” constituting the first-person locus from which reality beyond itself can be encountered.

##### Truth

Information or expression that faithfully conveys reality—actual or potential—in full accord with God’s essence, word, works, or creation across time, space, or imagination, without contradiction.

##### Will

Will is a person’s faculty, disposition or active calibrating of affinity at the intersection of internal valuation and perceived conditions of one or more present or future realities.
